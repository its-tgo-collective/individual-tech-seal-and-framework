## The Core Intent
This repository is not the intellectual property of a single individual. It is the collective intellectual property of all creators who suffer under the structural failures of the current copyright systems. The purpose is the protection of creators from exploitation, fair compensation, and the technical impossibility of unauthorized AI training.

## The Four Pillars of ITS Governance
1. **The Right to Fork (Protection against Tyranny):** No single entity can force a change. The minority always retains the right to fork the code and continue under the original conditions.
2. **The Swarm Consensus (Protection against Plutocracy):** Governance changes require a supermajority of hardware-bound, verified individuals. Financial power cannot buy protocol control.
3. **The Immutable Core Intent (Protection against Living Documents):** The fundamental rules (anti-AI, fair compensation) are mathematically locked and cannot be altered by corporate acquisitions.
4. **Voluntary Participation (Protection against Coercion):** The system offers a fair alternative that individuals choose to adopt.

## The Architecture (ITS & TGO)
*   **The Technical Executive (ITS):** A hardware-enforced licensing protocol. The Z6 Vector prevents unauthorized AI training and enforces compliance at the chip level. The Biography Fragment acts as a cryptographic key for visibility control.
*   **The Institutional Guardian (TGO):** A civil-society-funded network that acts as a "lightning rod" for politicians. It sets global standards, sanctions violations, and uses the Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) to force corporations to abandon vulnerable legacy code.

## Why this exists
Current licensing models fail to protect creators:
1. **Permissive licenses (MIT/Apache)** allow corporate free-riding and AI-scraping.
2. **Copyleft licenses (GPL)** are bypassed via SaaS and Tivoization.
3. **Living Documents** allow terms of service to be changed unilaterally.
4. **Hostile Buy-Outs** allow corporations to purchase creators and close the source.

ITS proposes a different angle:
- Bind the license rules to **hardware/execution environments** (TEE).
- Make execution **ephemeral** (one validation per use, then zeroize).
- Separate **working memory** (Operative Chain) from **proof archive** (Storage Chain).
- Enforce **immediate financial penalties** for unauthorized access.
- Mandate **Machine Unlearning** for unauthorized AI ingestion.
- Anchor the Work to a **Biography Fragment** (Cryptographic Aura) to make it unreadable for AI.
- Prevent **Hostile Buy-Outs** by making the Core Intent and Biography Fragment strictly non-transferable.
- Require **no central authority** for license validation.

## Ecosystem & Future Support (The TGO)
The Individual Tech Seal (ITS) is designed to be the technical and legal foundation for a broader ecosystem. We are currently developing the blueprint for a Tech-Governance-Organization (TGO). 

This independent, non-profit entity will act as the institutional counterpart to the ITS. If a Maintainer is attacked by a corporate entity or faces a critical security breach, the TGO will provide automated access to a pool of paid security experts and legal support. 

The ITS protects the code; the TGO protects the human behind it.
